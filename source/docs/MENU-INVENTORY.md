## New pages

### Talk
Hold the talk button near someone and speak.

- **Mic test** (action) — Records about two seconds and reports the level. Nothing is sent online or saved.
- **Microphone** (setting: `audio.micDeviceId`) — Which microphone to use. Default follows Windows.
- **Talk key (hold)** (setting: `controls.pttKey`) — Hold this key, speak, then let go.
- **Talk button (hold)** (setting: `controls.controllerPtt`) — Controller button to hold while you speak.
- **Language** (setting: `conversation.language`) — The language you speak and NPCs answer in.

### NPC Voices
How NPCs sound.

- **NPC voices** (setting: `audio.enabled`) — NPCs answer out loud. Subtitles still work when this is off.
- **Voice volume** (setting: `audio.masterVolume`) — One volume for every NPC voice.
- **Subtitles** (setting: `general.subtitles` + `world.subtitles`) — Show what NPCs say on screen.

### City Life
How busy the streets feel.

- **City mood** (action) — Quiet, Normal or Busy. Sets encounter rate, aggression and chatter together.
- **Street encounters** (setting: `world.enabled`) — Street events and gang activity near you.
- **Fights** (setting: `world.streetFights` + `world.turfWars`) — Street fights and gang turf wars.
- **Vehicle conversations** (setting: `conversation.vehicleEnabled`) — Talk to drivers and passengers through the window.
- **NPCs approach you** (setting: `conversation.approachEnabled`) — Let NPCs start a conversation. You can ignore them.

### Controls
Keys and buttons.

- **Menu key** (setting: `controls.menuKey`) — Opens this menu.
- **Menu button (hold with RB)** (setting: `controls.controllerMenu`) — Controller button held together with the modifier.
- **Panic key** (setting: `controls.panicKey`) — Stops every mod scene, voice and AI request at once.
- **Button hints** (setting: `general.hints`) — Show who you can talk to and which button to hold.
- **Button style** (setting: `controls.promptStyle`) — Xbox, PlayStation or keyboard labels. Auto follows your last input.

### AI & Status
Is everything connected?

- **Connection** (info) — Green when the companion and game are talking to each other.
- **AI in use** (info) — The AI model answering NPC conversations.
- **AI spend today** (info) — Estimated spend against the daily cap.
- **Test connection** (action) — Sends one small paid AI request within the daily cap.
- **Online AI** (setting: `ai.enabled`) — Off uses offline fallback lines, clearly labelled.

### Advanced
Everything else. The defaults are fine.

- **Restore defaults** (action) — Puts every setting back to its default, including keys.
- **Panic: stop everything** (action) — Cancels owned scenes, voices and AI. Also on the panic key.
- **Voice talk** (setting: `conversation.pushToTalk`) — Talk with your microphone. Audio is captured only while the button is held.
- **Tap to talk** (setting: `conversation.tapToTalk`) — Tap once to start listening; it stops after a short silence.
- **Typed talk (no mic)** (setting: `conversation.typedFallback`) — Typed text and the reply wheel for players without a microphone.
- **All settings** (action) — Every setting, searchable, plus the AI settings advisor.
- **Profiles** (action) — Save or apply a full set of settings.
- **Diagnostics** (action) — Self-tests, voice test, NPC inspector and a sanitized report.
